# Portfolio Website Context

This context defines the language used to present Sourav Chandhok's professional profile consistently across the portfolio website.

## Language

**New-Grad Software Engineer**:
The umbrella professional identity for Sourav, who has completed his computer-science academic requirements and is awaiting convocation while seeking full-time software engineering work. His portfolio evidence spans backend, full-stack, and AI applications.
_Avoid_: Student developer, aspiring developer, exploring opportunities

**Hiring Visitor**:
A recruiter, hiring manager, or technical reviewer evaluating Sourav for a full-time software engineering opportunity in Canada.
_Avoid_: User, audience, customer

**Focus Area**:
A software-engineering specialization supported by portfolio evidence. The current focus areas are Java/backend development, full-stack development, and AI systems.
_Avoid_: Target role, job title collection

**Featured Project**:
A project selected as primary evidence of engineering ability. The selected software projects are RoleImpact, JobHelperGuru, and YU Bazaar; public claims must be supported by project evidence.
_Avoid_: School assignment, sample project

**Research Case Study**:
A research-led project presented through its question, methodology, findings, limitations, and interactive evidence. The Toronto Airbnb Market Network is the current research case study.
_Avoid_: Data project, research paper project

**Portfolio Hub**:
The work-first homepage that gives a Hiring Visitor a fast overview and routes them to project evidence, the résumé, professional profiles, and contact options.
_Avoid_: Landing page, personal homepage

**Project Case Study**:
A focused account of a project's problem, Sourav's contribution, engineering decisions, evidence, and outcome. Featured Projects and the Research Case Study may each have one.
_Avoid_: Project detail, project description

**Professional Story**:
A concise personal introduction, supported by a photograph, that explains the motivation and perspective behind the work after the visitor has seen the primary evidence.
_Avoid_: Biography, life story
